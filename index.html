YOUR CREATIVE PARTNER

A passionate team of creatives ready to share Grand Maloekoe's story with the world.

Indonesia has captivated explorers for centuries. Beyond its well-known destinations lies an archipelago rich with maritime history, extraordinary biodiversity and cultures that continue to shape the identity of the nation today. After calling Indonesia home for the past ten years, we've come to appreciate just how many of these stories remain untold.

What immediately resonated with us about Grand Maloekoe is its ambition to honour that history, celebrate those cultures and reconnect people with parts of the archipelago that have been forgotten, overlooked or never truly understood. This project isn't centered around about promoting a yacht, building a website or filming a brand story, it's about inspiring exploration, curiosity and discovery.

That's a vision we're genuinely excited to be part of. We believe that together we can craft Grand Maloekoe into one of the world's leading expedition brands, creating an immersive brand experience that inspires people from around the world to see Indonesia through a completely new lens and Grand Maloekoe as the vessel to take you there.

INSPIRING WONDER & AWE

Sparking a profound sense of curiousity

BRAND CONCEPT 1

A modern take on history

Rather than recreating the past, this direction reinterprets it through a contemporary lens. Inspired by vintage expedition journals, maritime archives and the natural textures of the Indonesian archipelago, it balances timeless elegance with modern restraint. The result is a brand that feels sophisticated and premium, while remaining deeply connected to the spirit of exploration that defines Grand Maloekoe.

The visual language pairs refined serif typography with clean, contemporary layouts to create a sense of quiet confidence and editorial sophistication. An earthy palette of muted greens, warm sands, stone, parchment and deep ocean tones draws inspiration from Indonesia's natural landscapes, while tactile textures reminiscent of rattan, woven fibres, aged paper and historic maps add depth and authenticity. Subtle line work, cartographic details and understated graphic devices reference the history of exploration without feeling overly literal, creating a visual identity that feels both timeless and distinctly modern.

BRAND CONCEPT 2

Nostalgia for the lost art of exploration

This direction draws on the nostalgia of a slower, more intentional way of travelling. This isn't nostalgia for the past, but for the way travel used to make people feel. It's inspired by handwritten postcards, paper maps folded into backpacks, well-worn travel journals and the golden age of exploration, evoking the romance of adventures that felt disconnected from the modern world. The result is a brand identity that feels timeless, personal and emotionally resonant, inviting guests to rediscover the wonder of exploration through a contemporary lens.

The visual language combines expressive typography inspired by vintage travel magazines, classic yacht clubs and editorial publications with tactile paper textures, archival photography and understated graphic devices that feel collected over time. A warm, sun-faded palette of creams, faded blues, muted reds and weathered neutrals creates a distinctly nostalgic atmosphere, while bespoke insignias, stamps and editorial layouts add a sense of heritage and collectability. Inspired by iconic adventure brands such as Patagonia and historic expedition publications, the identity feels both familiar and enduring, capturing not just the aesthetic of exploration, but the feeling of it.

PHOTOTOGRAPHY & VIDEOGRAPHY

A cinematic visual language inspired by exploration.

More than a brand film. The beginning of a larger story.

Our recommendation is to begin by creating a flagship cinematic brand film that introduces audiences to the world of Grand Maloekoe—its philosophy, its spirit of exploration and the extraordinary diversity of the Indonesian archipelago.

Rather than viewing this as a standalone production, we'd approach it as the foundation of a much broader storytelling platform. The narrative, visual language and production methodology would all be designed with the intention of evolving into a future documentary series, where each expedition becomes its own chapter exploring the history, cultures, wildlife and remarkable people that define a different region of Indonesia.

The initial production would focus on Komodo, capturing the core footage needed for the flagship brand film while simultaneously laying the creative foundations for future episodes. This approach maximises the value of every production, ensuring today's investment continues to support the brand's storytelling for years to come.

Creative Direction

The film will lean into a cinematic documentary style, driven by an immersive soundscape and thoughtful visual storytelling. Rather than relying solely on sweeping drone shots, we'll incorporate a wide variety of visual techniques including macro cinematography, dynamic camera movement, underwater sequences, timelapses and detailed close-ups to create a richer, more engaging viewing experience.

Importantly, the production will be approached as the foundation of a broader storytelling platform. While the immediate deliverable is a flagship brand film, the visual language, narration and production methodology will all be designed so they can naturally evolve into a future documentary series, where each destination becomes its own chapter.

Alongside the primary documentary-style film, we'll also produce a shorter Director's Cut designed specifically for trade shows, exhibitions, website banners and social media. This version will focus on striking visuals and pacing, allowing it to communicate the brand effectively in environments where audio may not be available.

Our Production Approach

To maximise the value of the Komodo production, we'll capture a series of universal "bridge" scenes that are not location-specific. For example, we may film the host preparing dive equipment, stepping into the tender, entering the water or exploring the yacht through a series of intimate close-up shots. These sequences can then transition seamlessly into high-quality footage from Raja Ampat or other destinations sourced from Grand Maloekoe's existing archive, Kelapa Creative's media library, our trusted network of filmmakers and carefully selected licensed footage.

While this approach won't provide the same depth or authenticity as filming every destination firsthand, it creates a cohesive narrative that effectively showcases the breadth of Grand Maloekoe's itineraries for the flagship brand film, while also establishing a production framework that can be expanded as future destination films are commissioned.

WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT

An immersive digital experience

Rather than creating a traditional luxury travel website, our vision is to build an immersive digital experience that invites visitors into the world of Grand Maloekoe. Rich cinematography, thoughtful animation and interactive storytelling work together to create a journey that feels exploratory rather than transactional, encouraging visitors to uncover more with every scroll rather than simply browse information.

Beautiful design should never come at the expense of performance. Behind the rich visual experience, the website will be technically sophisticated, ensuring fast loading times, strong search engine performance and an intuitive user experience across every device. The result is a website that feels as refined and considered as the journeys themselves.




Key Features

The website experience extends beyond beautiful visuals. Interactive destination guides allow guests to explore itineraries through destinations, interests and experiences rather than traditional packages. Editorial-style journals mirror the aesthetic of the printed expedition publication, while animated maps and bespoke illustrations help bring each voyage to life. Throughout the experience, subtle motion design and carefully considered interactions create the feeling that there is always another story waiting to be discovered.



Alongside the guest experience, we'll develop practical tools that support the sales process, including a custom itinerary builder that allows the Grand Maloekoe team to create beautifully presented, interactive proposals tailored to each client. Rather than sending static PDFs, every proposal becomes an extension of the brand experience, reinforcing the same sense of exploration and craftsmanship found throughout the website.

Project Timeline

The project has been intentionally structured into individual workstreams, allowing Grand Maloekoe to prioritise initiatives while maintaining one cohesive creative vision. Each stage builds upon the previous, ensuring the brand identity, storytelling, digital experience and launch assets evolve together rather than as independent projects.

Following appointment, we'll begin with a collaborative discovery workshop to refine the strategic direction, priorities and project roadmap. From there, each workstream builds upon the last, allowing the brand, website, editorial publication and launch assets to evolve together as one cohesive ecosystem.

Phase 1 — Discovery & Creative Direction

Workshops, stakeholder interviews, research, creative exploration and refinement of the overall brand vision, positioning and storytelling direction.

Phase 2 — Brand Identity

Development of the visual identity, creative direction, brand guidelines and the core design system that will inform every future touchpoint.

Phase 3 — Digital & Editorial Experience

Website design and development, editorial publication design, supporting collateral and user experience design, ensuring every touchpoint feels cohesive and aligned.

Phase 4 — Production

Photography, cinematography and content production, followed by post-production, editing and the creation of launch assets including the flagship brand film.

Phase 5 — Launch & Ongoing Growth

Launch event, website deployment and the beginning of an ongoing creative partnership through content creation, social media and future documentary episodes as the Grand Maloekoe story continues to evolve.
