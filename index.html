YOUR CREATIVE PARTNER

A passionate team of creatives ready to share Grand Maloeke's story with the world.

Indonesia has captivated explorers for centuries. Beyond its well-known destinations lies an archipelago rich with maritime history, extraordinary biodiversity and cultures that continue to shape the identity of the nation today. After calling Indonesia home for the past ten years, we've come to appreciate just how many of these stories remain untold.

What immediately resonated with us about Grand Maloekoe is its ambition to honour that history, celebrate those cultures and reconnect people with parts of the archipelago that have been forgotten, overlooked or never truly understood. This project isn't centered around about promoting a yacht, building a website or filming a brand story, it's about inspiring exploration, curiosity and discovery.

That's a vision we're genuinely excited to be part of. We believe that together we can craft Grand Maloekoe into one of the world's leading expedition brands, creating an immersive brand experience that inspires people from around the world to see Indonesia through a completely new lens and Grand Maloekoe as the vessel to take you there.

BRAND CONCEPT 1

A modern take on history

Rather than recreating the past, this direction reinterprets it through a contemporary lens. Inspired by vintage expedition journals, maritime archives and the natural textures of the Indonesian archipelago, it balances timeless elegance with modern restraint. The result is a brand that feels sophisticated and premium, while remaining deeply connected to the spirit of exploration that defines Grand Maloekoe.

The visual language pairs refined serif typography with clean, contemporary layouts to create a sense of quiet confidence and editorial sophistication. An earthy palette of muted greens, warm sands, stone, parchment and deep ocean tones draws inspiration from Indonesia's natural landscapes, while tactile textures reminiscent of rattan, woven fibres, aged paper and historic maps add depth and authenticity. Subtle line work, cartographic details and understated graphic devices reference the history of exploration without feeling overly literal, creating a visual identity that feels both timeless and distinctly modern.

BRAND CONCEPT 2

Nostalgia for the lost art of exploration

This direction draws on the nostalgia of a slower, more intentional way of travelling. This isn't nostalgia for the past, but for the way travel used to make people feel. It's inspired by handwritten postcards, paper maps folded into backpacks, well-worn travel journals and the golden age of exploration, evoking the romance of adventures that felt disconnected from the modern world. The result is a brand identity that feels timeless, personal and emotionally resonant, inviting guests to rediscover the wonder of exploration through a contemporary lens.

The visual language combines expressive typography inspired by vintage travel magazines, classic yacht clubs and editorial publications with tactile paper textures, archival photography and understated graphic devices that feel collected over time. A warm, sun-faded palette of creams, faded blues, muted reds and weathered neutrals creates a distinctly nostalgic atmosphere, while bespoke insignias, stamps and editorial layouts add a sense of heritage and collectability. Inspired by iconic adventure brands such as Patagonia and historic expedition publications, the identity feels both familiar and enduring, capturing not just the aesthetic of exploration, but the feeling of it.
